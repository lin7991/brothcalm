📖 Food Therapy Theory

The framework behind the food: five flavors, food energetics, seasonal eating.

Start with the core ideas

Recipes tell you what to cook. Theory explains why a dish suits one person and not another. This is the reference shelf for the ideas BrothCalm uses everywhere else: the five flavors, the four natures of food, seasonal eating, body constitution, and the traditional organ clock.

Each guide separates what the Chinese food-therapy tradition says from what modern nutrition research can and cannot confirm, so you can use the framework without mistaking it for medical advice.
