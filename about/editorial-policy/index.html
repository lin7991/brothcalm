Editorial Policy & Sources

Quick answer

BrothCalm is an educational resource about Chinese food therapy (食疗). Our editorial goal is to explain traditional ideas clearly while keeping the difference between TCM tradition and modern clinical evidence visible.

BrothCalm is an educational resource about Chinese food therapy (食疗). Our editorial goal is to explain traditional ideas clearly while keeping the difference between TCM tradition and modern clinical evidence visible.

How we research

Articles begin with the food, tea, recipe, or TCM concept named in the title. We consult established English and Chinese-language references where available, compare traditional descriptions, and identify preparation details that a home cook can follow. We do not present a traditional claim as a proven medical treatment.

How we write

  • Traditional claims are attributed with wording such as “in TCM,” “traditionally used,” or “is believed to.”
  • Practical recipes state ingredients, preparation, serving guidance, and timing where appropriate.
  • Safety notes identify situations that warrant professional advice, including pregnancy, medication use, allergies, and persistent or serious symptoms.
  • Articles are written for general education and do not diagnose, treat, or replace qualified medical care.

Sources and attribution

Our background framework is the traditional Chinese dietary-therapy tradition, including the classical food and medicine ideas associated with texts such as the Huangdi Neijing and Shiliao Bencao. Classical references describe historical theory; they are not the same as modern clinical evidence. Where an article makes a modern scientific statement, it should be supported by an identifiable research or public-health source.

Updates and corrections

We review pages when preparation details, safety information, or the underlying evidence changes. If you spot an inaccurate statement, broken link, or unsafe instruction, please contact contact@brothcalm.com with the page URL and the suggested correction.

Scope

BrothCalm covers food, beverages, culinary herbs, seasonal eating, and TCM food-therapy theory. It does not provide individualized medical advice. See our medical disclaimer for more detail.
